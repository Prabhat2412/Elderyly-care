Relevance to ElderCare AI System SLIDE 7 / 8
What to INCLUDE
✓ Caregiver Alert System
Notify family when elderly user does not complete a check-in —
mirrors Life360's missed-event notification model.
✓ Geofence / Places Logic
Trigger alerts when elderly user leaves a defined safe zone (home)
unexpectedly.
✓ SOS / Emergency Button
A simple, one-tap emergency button visible on the home screen
for immediate family notification.
✓ Real-Time Location Share
Optional live location sharing with designated family members for
safety assurance.
✓ Simple Invite System
Allow family members to join via a link — no technical setup
required.
What to AVOID
✕ Constant GPS Tracking
24/7 surveillance may feel intrusive and disrespectful of elderly users'
autonomy and privacy.
✕ Driver Reports
Irrelevant for most elderly care use cases and adds unnecessary
complexity to the interface.
✕ Complex Circle Management
Admin roles, permissions, and member management screens are too
technical for elderly primary users.
✕ Crime Alerts
Can cause anxiety and fear — counterproductive for elderly users'
mental wellbeing.
✕ Premium Paywalls
Critical safety features should not be locked behind subscriptions in
an elder care context.

Key Takeaways
Life360 → ElderCare AI System
01 Alert Architecture
Life360's instant family notification model is the blueprint for
our missed check-in alert system.
02 Geofencing
The Places feature shows how location boundaries can trigger
automatic safety notifications — applicable for home-based
elder monitoring.
03 One-Tap SOS
A prominent emergency button that requires minimal
interaction is essential for elderly users in distress.
04 Privacy Balance
Constant tracking must be replaced with consent-based, opt-in
check-ins to respect elderly users' dignity and independence.
