MyTherapy
The app begins with a consent screen before collecting any information. Once accepted, users are guided
through a step-by-step setup — entering their name, followed by their birth year and gender. After the
profile is created, users can personalise their experience by selecting a reminder tone and granting
notification permissions.

-- 1 of 8 --

Once onboarding is complete, users are taken to the main dashboard where the core wellness categories
are presented, each of which can be individually set up and customised.
Categories
• Medication — for setting up and managing medication reminders and tracking
• Measurements — for logging and monitoring health metrics
• Activities — for tracking daily physical and lifestyle activities
• Mood & Symptom — for recording emotional state and any symptoms experienced
Opening the medication section begins with asking what time the user typically takes their first medication
of the day, helping the app establish a personalised reminder schedule from the start.
Adding a Medication Users have multiple ways to add a medication:
• Barcode scan — scan the medication packaging directly
• Search by name — look up the medication manually
• Scan a medication plan — scan an existing prescription or medication plan document
When adding via search, the app walks the user through a detailed setup:
• Who it's for — the medication can be assigned to a specific profile
• Frequency — how often the medication is taken
• Reminder time — when the user would like to be reminded to take it
• Refill reminder — option to set an alert when inventory is running low
• Reason for taking — the symptom or condition the medication is addressing

The measurements section offers a wide range of health metrics that users can track. These include
common vitals and body measurements such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, and
more, giving users a comprehensive way to monitor their physical health over time.
When adding a measurement, users can configure how often they want to take a reading and set a specific
time to be reminded — helping them build a consistent health monitoring routine

-- 3 of 8 --

The activities section presents a broad range of daily living activities organised in alphabetical order, making
it easy to find and select what is relevant to the user. Similar to measurements, once an activity is selected,
users can configure how frequently it should be done and set a preferred reminder time to stay on track.

The mood and symptom section allows users to log their emotional state and any symptoms they are
experiencing. Similar to the other sections, users can set how frequently they would like to be prompted
and choose a preferred time for the reminder, helping them maintain a consistent daily check-in habit.
Once all categories are configured, users can how many reminders have been set under each category.
When satisfied, they can press "All Set" to complete the setup and move into the app.

-- 6 of 8 --

After setup, users land on the main home screen which displays a date bar at the top for easy navigation
across days. A welcome message greets the user, followed by a clear overview of all their daily plans and
scheduled reminders for the day.
The app is organised through a bottom navigation bar with the following sections:
• Today — displays all reminders due for the day along with quick options to add a doctor
appointment, set a reminder, or document other entries as well.
• Progress — allows users to track their overall adherence and health progress over time
• Support — features the Mighty chatbot, powered by OpenAI, where users can have health-related
conversations. This section also allows users to add a pharmacy and healthcare professionals to
their profile
• Treatment — a dedicated section for managing the full treatment plan, including adding
medications, checking drug interactions, editing reminders, and accessing an e-prescription feature
