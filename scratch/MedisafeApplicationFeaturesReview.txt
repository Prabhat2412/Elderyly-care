Medisafe
The app begins with a simple onboarding process where users are asked to create an account
or continue as a guest. Users can enter basic details such as their name and preferred time
format. The app then prompts users to add their first medication, guiding them step-by-step to
ensure reminders are set up correctly. Notification permissions are requested early to enable
timelyalerts.

Once onboarding is complete
Users are taken to the main dashboard, which presents their daily medication schedule in a
clear and organized timeline view.
Categories
• Medications — for adding, managing, and tracking medication intake
• Schedule — displays daily and upcoming doses in a timeline format
• Medfriends — allows users to connect with family or caregivers for support
• Reports — provides adherence reports and medication history
Adding a Medication
Users have multiple ways to add a medication:
• Search by name — find medication from a database
• Manual entry — add custom medication details
• Scan option — scan medication packaging (available in some regions)
When adding a medication, the app walks the user through a detailed setup:
• Dosage — amount and unit of medication
• Frequency — daily, weekly, or custom schedule
• Time — specific times for reminders
• Instructions — before/after food or special notes
• Start and end date — duration of medication
Schedule Section
The schedule interface displays medications in a chronological timeline for the day. Each
entry includes:
• Medication name
• Dosage
• Time
• Status (taken, missed, skipped)
Users can mark medications as taken directly from this screen, making it quick and
interactive.
Medfriends Section
This feature allows users to add trusted contacts (family members or caregivers) who can:

• Receive alerts if a dose is missed
• Monitor adherence remotely
This adds a social and safety layer to the app.
Reports Section
The reports section provides insights into medication adherence over time. Users can view:
• Daily and weekly adherence rates
• Missed doses
• Medication history
Reports can also be shared with healthcare professionals.
Home Screen
After setup, users land on the main home screen which shows:
• A daily timeline of medications
• Progress indicators for completed doses
• Quick action buttons to add medication
Navigation
The app is organized through a bottom navigation bar with the following sections:
• Today — shows all medications scheduled for the day
• Medications — list of all added medications
• Medfriends — caregiver and family connections
• More — settings, reports, and additional features
